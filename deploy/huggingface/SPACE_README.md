# Mutual Fund Facts Assistant (ICICI Prudential)

A retrieval-augmented FAQ assistant that answers **factual** questions about five ICICI Prudential mutual fund schemes (Silver ETF FoF, Large Cap, Large & Mid Cap, Flexicap, and Smallcap) from 17 official AMC and SEBI documents. Every answer cites one official source and shows when it was last updated. It refuses investment advice, return calculations, and personal information.

> **Facts-only. No investment advice.** Independent portfolio project, not affiliated with or endorsed by ICICI Prudential AMC or SEBI.

Source code, design, and evaluation: <https://github.com/aryansinghdev01/icici-mf-facts-assistant>

The first request after the Space has been idle can take a minute while it wakes up.
